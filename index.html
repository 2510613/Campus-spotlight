ABOUT CAMPUS SPOTLIGHT

Make room for
big ideas.

Students and emerging creators often need cameras, lighting, microphones and suitable spaces to produce quality content. Buying equipment can be expensive, while commercial studios may be difficult to access.

Campus Spotlight aims to make content creation more accessible by bringing bookable studio spaces and shared equipment closer to university communities.

OUR MISSION

To make professional content creation more affordable, convenient and accessible for students and creative individuals.
